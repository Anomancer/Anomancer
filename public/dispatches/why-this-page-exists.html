Why does this page exist?

Dispatches are ANOMANCER's explanation layer: one idea, problem or built system at a time, translated into human language.

I build a lot of things quickly, and from the outside the whole thing can turn into a strange map with no obvious entrance.

Dispatches are the bridge. Here I unpack one idea, problem or structure at a time in human language. If something catches your interest, the text can lead to a demo, source code, or a deeper layer inside Black Hole Core Observatory.

The goal is not to make everything simple. The goal is to make an entrance.

What lives here?

  • AI agents and agent orchestration
  • building the TEE language
  • semantics and claim structure
  • build logs and experiments
  • research notes
  • field notes about what broke and why
